# Garrett Audet

I’m Garrett Audet. I build software around practical problems, with a background spanning computer science, business, growth analytics, and applied research.

My work includes developer tools, AI-assisted information retrieval, and workflow automation. I’m interested in both how a system works and whether it solves a problem worth solving.

I’m currently a Schwarzman Scholar at Tsinghua University and host 10 Minutes in Tech, exploring emerging technology and its business implications.

## Selected projects

- [Stock Price Prediction Leveraging Reddit](https://github.com/GarrettAudet/Stock-Price-Prediction-Leveraging-Reddit) · Research exploring Reddit sentiment, author trust, and sliding-window methods for stock-price prediction. Associated with IEEE Big Data 2021.
- [Personal Portfolio](https://github.com/GarrettAudet/PersonalWebsite) · My portfolio of software projects, applied research, and work at the intersection of technology and business.
- [Blackboard Search Extension](https://github.com/GarrettAudet/blackboard-search-extension) · A local-first Chrome extension that searches course materials and answers questions with links to supporting sources.
- [Job Discovery Newsletter](https://github.com/GarrettAudet/schwarzman-opportunity-radar) · A Python pipeline that discovers job postings, filters and ranks them against configurable criteria, and prepares a focused newsletter.
- [MarText — SMS Marketplace](https://github.com/GarrettAudet/LegacyMarText) · A team-built marketplace for posting and finding local listings through SMS, without requiring mobile internet access.
